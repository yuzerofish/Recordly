Recordly Cartoon Face Layer
---------------------------
The white eyes, black pupils, and toothy smile are original deterministic
Canvas drawings implemented in src/lib/webcamEffects/cartoonFace.ts. Their
visual direction was informed by a user-provided reference image. No pixels,
photographic subject, or background from that reference are included.

The person segmentation and face landmark models run locally. Recordly does
not upload webcam frames, segmentation masks, or facial landmarks for this
effect.
